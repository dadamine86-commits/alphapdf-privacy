2. Documents and files

AlphaPDF is used to read, scan, convert, compress, merge, split, and edit PDFs on the device. Document content is processed mainly locally.

AlphaPDF does not operate a server that receives your PDFs in order to read, scan, convert, compress, or run the tools described in the app.

  • Temporary files may exist in the app’s storage or cache while a task is in progress.
  • A file you explicitly save or share may be placed in Downloads, or in a location you choose.
  • Clearing the app cache does not delete files you have saved elsewhere.

The app uses the Internet for advertising, consent, and Google Play. That does not mean the content of your PDFs is sent to an AlphaPDF server.

3. History, preferences, and quotas

Some information stays on the device so the app can work:

  • Local history: a limited number of recent actions, including the display name, action type, date, size if available, and the file location or identifier. History is not designed to store document content. Clearing it removes the list, not files saved elsewhere.
  • Preferences: for example theme, Pro purchase status, and other choices needed for the app to function.
  • Free-version counters: for each covered tool, the number of successful operations in the 24-hour window and, where relevant, when a temporary Pass ends.

4. Permissions

Depending on what you do, Android may request or use:

  • Internet and network state: ads, the consent form when it is required, and communication with Google Play for purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob.
  • Camera: only for the scanner. The camera is not required to read a PDF that is already on the device.
  • Google Play Billing: to offer and verify AlphaPDF Pro.

Documents are generally chosen, shared, or saved by you through the system screens. The current version of the app does not request a general permission to browse all storage on the phone.

5. Free version, advertising, and the Pass

The free version keeps the main features. It is funded in part by advertising and by quotas. AlphaPDF Pro removes quotas and ads. The free version is not designed to deliberately reduce features or quality in order to push a purchase.

5.1 Quotas

Without AlphaPDF Pro, each of the following tools has its own quota of 10 successful operations per 24-hour window:

  • Scanner
  • Convert
  • Compress
  • Merge / Split
  • PDF tools

Opening and reading a PDF does not use this quota. An operation counts only when a final result is created successfully. Cancelling, an error, or simply opening a file does not use quota. Each tool’s 24-hour window starts with its first successful operation.

5.2 Google AdMob

The free version may show Google AdMob ads: banners, interstitials according to the app’s rules, and rewarded videos that you choose to start.

Google and its partners may process, for their own purposes, technical information related to advertising. Depending on the situation and your choices, this can include information about the device and the app, the advertising ID, delivery, measurement, and fraud-prevention data, and, if you have consented where that is required, information used for personalization.

AlphaPDF does not control the detail of Google’s processing. Google’s own advertising and privacy information applies to those services.

5.3 Consent (Google UMP)

Where the law requires it, AlphaPDF uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show the consent form and to let you review those choices. Privacy options can also be opened from the app settings when they are available.

If ad requests are not allowed, banners, interstitials, and the rewarded Pass may be unavailable. Free-version quotas can still apply. You can keep using the app within those limits, or subscribe to AlphaPDF Pro if the offer is available on your Google Play account.

5.4 Rewarded video and the 10-minute Pass

When a tool’s quota reaches zero, the app may offer a rewarded ad for that tool. The Pass starts only after Google actually grants the reward. An interrupted, dismissed, or unrewarded video does not start the Pass.

  • The Pass lasts 10 minutes.
  • It applies only to the tool for which the reward was earned.
  • It does not reset or refill the quota.
  • During those 10 minutes, use of that tool is not limited by the quota.
  • The Pass is not AlphaPDF Pro. It does not remove advertising as a whole. Some interstitials tied to opening that tool may not be shown during the Pass. Free-version banners may still appear.
  • Availability depends on consent, ad inventory, and the Google service working at that moment.

6. AlphaPDF Pro

AlphaPDF Pro is a purchase handled by Google Play. While it is active, it removes per-tool quotas and the app’s ads. The price, duration, and type of offer (for example a one-time purchase or a subscription, if more than one plan is offered) are those shown in the app and on Google Play at the time of purchase.

AlphaPDF does not receive your card details. Google Play handles payment, billing, purchase restoration, and refund requests under its own rules.

The app keeps on the device the technical status needed to know whether Pro is active, and it checks that status with Google Play.

7. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service: reading, PDF tools, scanning, preferences, local history, quotas, and recognition of the Pro purchase.
  • Consent: advertising and related choices where the law requires it, through the Google UMP form.
  • Legitimate interests: app security, preventing abuse of quotas and the Pass, and keeping the app working.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires a notice, a response to a rights request, or another compliance step.

8. Recipients

Document content is not sent to an AlphaPDF server for the app’s tools. Third-party services are still involved for other purposes:

  • Google AdMob, to deliver and measure ads;
  • Google UMP, for the consent message and advertising privacy choices;
  • Google Play, to distribute the app and handle Pro purchases.

Those services have their own policies. Google components already on the device may also be involved in advertising, consent, or billing.

9. Retention

  • Temporary app files: for the duration of the task, then until they are deleted, the cache is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, as applicable.
  • Files you saved elsewhere: they remain in the location you chose until you delete them. The app does not remove them by clearing its cache or history.
  • Local history and preferences: until you clear them in the app, reset them, or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising choices: according to Google UMP and the storage those tools use.
  • Pro purchase: for the duration of the Google Play offer and for as long as the technical information needed to recognize it is required.

10. Your rights

Depending on your situation and applicable law, including the GDPR, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can change some choices in the app, open advertising privacy options when they are offered, delete files or local history, and uninstall the app. To send a request to the publisher, email dadamine86@gmail.com. A reply may ask for the information reasonably needed to identify you and understand the request. You may also contact the French data protection authority (CNIL).

Processing carried out by Google as controller of its own services can also be raised with Google, through the tools Google provides.

11. Age

AlphaPDF asks you to confirm that you are 16 or older. Advertising features may also be limited under the applicable consent rules. If you represent a minor and believe the app was used contrary to those rules, contact us.

12. Security

The app limits Android automatic backup of its data and processes documents on the device first. No technical measure can guarantee the complete absence of risk, file loss, or unwanted access, especially if the device is compromised or if you share a document yourself.
