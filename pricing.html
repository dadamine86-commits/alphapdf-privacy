Pricing

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page describes the free version, the Pass, and AlphaPDF Pro. The price of a purchase is the price shown in the app and on Google Play when you confirm it. This website does not charge any payment.

Offer What you get Price
Free Main features, with a separate quota for each tool and advertising. €0
10-minute Pass Temporary use of one tool without its quota limit, after a rewarded ad is actually granted. €0
AlphaPDF Pro No per-tool quota and no ads in the app. Shown on Google Play

Free version

€0 — with ads
  • Unlimited PDF reading: opening a PDF does not use quota.
  • 10 successful operations per tool per 24-hour window.
  • Tools are counted separately: Scanner, Convert, Compress, Merge / Split, PDF tools.
  • An operation counts only when a final result is created successfully.
  • Banners and interstitials according to the app’s rules, after consent where it is required.

10-minute Pass

€0 — 10 minutes, one tool

When a tool’s quota is used up, you can start a rewarded video. The Pass starts only if Google grants the reward.

  • It lasts 10 minutes and applies only to the tool you chose.
  • It does not refill the quota.
  • It does not remove advertising as a whole and does not replace Pro.
  • It may be unavailable if advertising consent is missing or if no ad is offered.

AlphaPDF Pro

See in app — Google Play
  • Removes tool quotas.
  • Removes the app’s ads.
  • It is not a plan in which free features are deliberately degraded: Pro mainly provides an ad-free experience and use that is not limited by quotas.
  • Purchase, any subscription, restoration, and refunds follow Google Play rules. The price is shown before payment.

Good to know

  • No purchase is completed on this page.
  • More than one plan may be offered in the app. Only the plans visible on your Google Play account can be bought.
